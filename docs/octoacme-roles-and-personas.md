# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile practices, remove impediments, and ensure the team follows agreed-upon processes. They coach the team on self-organization and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, daily standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Shield the team from external disruptions
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track and communicate team velocity and metrics
- Foster collaboration between Product Owner, developers, and stakeholders

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Ensure sustainable pace and prevent burnout
- Build team capability and self-organization
- Maintain transparency and visibility into progress

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker tracking
- Sprint planning and retrospective sessions
- Impediment escalations to leadership
- Velocity reports and burndown charts

### Interaction Notes
- Works closely with Project Manager on scheduling and resource planning
- Partners with Product Manager on backlog refinement and priorities
- Supports Developers by clearing obstacles and improving workflows
- Collaborates with Business Analyst on requirement clarification

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and ensure the product delivers a positive user experience. They bridge user requirements with technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Define user flows and interaction patterns
- Establish and maintain design systems and style guides
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Validate designs through user feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction and improve user task completion rates
- Maintain design consistency across the product
- Advocate for user needs in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and reviews with the team
- User research findings and persona documentation
- Design specifications and handoff materials for developers
- Usability test reports and recommendations

### Interaction Notes
- Partners with Product Manager on feature prioritization based on user needs
- Collaborates with Developers on technical constraints and implementation
- Works with Business Analyst to understand user workflows and requirements
- Coordinates with Technical Writer on UI copy and help documentation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business stakeholders and technical teams by eliciting requirements, analyzing processes, and ensuring solutions meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Analyze current processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Create functional specifications and user stories
- Facilitate requirements workshops with stakeholders
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs
- Support change management and user adoption

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance criteria
- Identify risks and dependencies early in the process
- Facilitate alignment between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story refinement
- Stakeholder interviews and workshop facilitation
- Impact analysis and feasibility assessments
- Acceptance testing and validation reports

### Interaction Notes
- Collaborates with Product Manager on defining success metrics and priorities
- Works with UX Designer to understand user workflows and pain points
- Partners with Developers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Supports Technical Writer in documenting business processes and use cases

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create clear, accurate documentation that helps users, developers, and stakeholders understand and use the product effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Write and maintain user documentation, API references, and guides
- Create onboarding materials and tutorials
- Document processes, procedures, and troubleshooting steps
- Review and edit technical content for clarity and accuracy
- Ensure documentation is up-to-date with product changes
- Collaborate on information architecture and content strategy

### Goals
- Reduce support burden through comprehensive self-service documentation
- Enable users to accomplish tasks independently
- Maintain documentation quality and consistency
- Improve time-to-productivity for new users and team members

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with subject matter experts
- Content feedback sessions with users and support teams
- Release notes and change documentation
- Style guide updates and documentation standards

### Interaction Notes
- Works with Product Manager to understand feature roadmap and messaging
- Collaborates with Developers to document technical specifications and APIs
- Partners with UX Designer on in-product help and UI copy
- Coordinates with Business Analyst to document business processes and workflows
